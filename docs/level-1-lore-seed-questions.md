# Level 1 lore decisions and remaining seeds

## Decided

1. Level 1 is an old delver-orientation course. It now operates as a small dungeon ecosystem rather than a clean sequence of lessons.
2. The floor stays light on exposition. One damaged intake ledger near the entry hints at the course's original purpose, its floor boss, and the reuse of casualties.
3. Stitches' stray memory belongs to a donor. Dead adventurers provide abundant bodies and minds; suitable donors can be restored, mind-wiped, rebuilt, and reused.
4. The Two-Headed King is the real boss of Level 1, whether or not he understands the course that appointed him.
5. Creatures can be assembled or made to appear when the dungeon has the correct parts. This supports the ecosystem and replacement populations without exposing a sentient system.
6. The Blue Lightsaber display carries a memory the nearby machine caught from the soul of a dying off-world knight beside it.
7. The spectral camp is a past expedition that did not feel ready to stop playing: "ghosts of gamers past," older adventurers who kept the expedition going.
8. The classroom teacher sincerely believes they are a great teacher. The room is not presently an official Dweomercore annex.
9. Completing Level 1 should feel like surviving a joke that is less than cruel but deliberately unpleasant.

## What an iconic legacy-item callback means

A legacy item is a memorable object that later changes an authored scene. An iconic callback means one strong payoff instead of many minor menu options: the Wife-Beater surviving the king's killing curse, the Blue Lightsaber awakening the dead knight's memory, or the Ball Cap provoking one consequential bad idea.

## Still open

1. Are the Three Lords and Undertakers a real faction with history below Level 1, or a local club using borrowed imagery?
2. How much of the donor process should players understand on Level 1 beyond the ledger's euphemistic columns?
3. What concrete secret should the spectral expedition reveal?
4. What does a missed classroom answer physically summon: an altar, Professor Grin, or both as one transformation?
5. Should the map-reset joke be a rare line from the spectral expedition, or wait until map reset can become a real mechanic?
